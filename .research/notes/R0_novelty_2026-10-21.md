## 1. Exactly which statement was checked

For EVERY nonempty finite A subset of the positive integers, with distinct
frequencies and coefficients exactly1, set n=|A|, F_A(z)=sum_A z^a,
f_A=Re F_A and M(A)=-min_circle f_A. Assume the ACTUAL all-circle
condition M(A)<=sqrt(n), FIX lambda=2sqrt(n), and put

    p_A=1/(lambda+F_A), q_A=Im p_A,
    Q_A=<q_A²>, H_A=<2f_A q_A²>.

The provisional quantitative assertion is

    R0: H_A/Q_A<=-sqrt(n)/100.

Equivalently, with g=lambda²<|p_A|²>-1>0 and
T0=sum_(a in A)[z^a]p_A², it asserts

    tau=-lambda*T0/(2g)<=1-1/400.

The exact constant is a working target. The structural question is
whether there exists ANY absolute eta>0 such that tau<=1-eta for all
these inputs. This is a prescribed-witness sufficient conjecture for
Chowla; a converse from Chowla to this particular witness bound is not
established. The recorded R0 would imply M(A)>=sqrt(n)/200.

## 3. Established analytic machinery versus the actual open claim

The normalized function C_A=1+2F_A/lambda has Re C_A>=0 under lowM.
Its Cayley transform is

    zeta_A=(C_A-1)/(C_A+1)=F_A/(lambda+F_A),
    p_A=(1-zeta_A)/lambda, q_A=-Im zeta_A/lambda.

Positive-real-part/Caratheodory functions and their Schur transforms are
explicit in [Bultheel et al., Generalizations of orthogonal polynomials,
JCAM179(2005), printed p61](https://medialibrary.uantwerpen.be/oldcontent/container2652/files/papers/Bu_Cu_ea_gen_05.pdf).
The source's Schur function divides the Cayley transform by z; our
zeta is z times that function. Hardy-space coefficient identities and
Rayleigh quotients are also established frameworks. Using this transform
is not itself a novelty claim. The
separate [advisor antecedent review](advisor_R0_novelty_2026-10-21.md)
records classical primary sources and the limits of the comparison.

The candidate new mathematical CONTENT is the uniform upper estimate
on the SELECTED coefficients of p_A², tied simultaneously to exact
Booleanity, n, the fixed lambda and the actual lowM hypothesis:

    -sum_A[z^a]p_A² <= 2(1-eta)g/lambda.

The generic analytic identities do not prove this estimate; the saved
sign audit obtains only a lower bound on tau from positivity. Three
finite certified successes and the elementary g>9/16 sufficient branch
do not establish it. A new notation or provisional constant does not
make a substantive result; the significance would come from proving a
uniform bound or identifying a mathematically useful counterexample.
